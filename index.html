Wickerwood

IN DEVELOPMENT · PC

A folk-horror defence game. By day you build, raid what the woods are hiding and talk to the few who are left. By night the masked swarm comes out of the trees, and you hold the village fire. Night after night.

By day

  • Build houses and farms, and place the towers you earn each dawn anywhere on open ground
  • Raid guarded camps, caves and a ruined castle for gear, relics and gold. Deep in each cave a warden guards the chest
  • Find the Heart of the Wood, where wicker figures stand round the Hollow Oak
  • Trade with Captain Wren when her ship comes in; after a storm, raid the wreck
  • Talk to a smith, a merchant, a hermit and a ghost for clues to legendary weapons

By night

  • Fight with sword, spear, axe, bow and crossbow, a shield and spells
  • Block, parry, break their guard, and lock on to the one that matters
  • A boss every fifth night. Sometimes the one in the boss's mask is only pretending
  • Thirty kinds of foe, a different warband each night, each with its own weaknesses to learn

New in the wood

Captain Wren's ship, and wrecks after a storm. Starry nights over a village that lights its windows. Caves that go deeper, each with a warden. And the Heart of the Wood. Real music now too, by day and by night.
